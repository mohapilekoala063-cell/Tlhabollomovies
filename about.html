About TLHABOLLO

OUR STORY

Welcome to TLHABOLLO

TLHABOLLO is a movie website developed by a group of Limkokwing University of Creative Technology Lesotho students. The idea came about when we realised that there were no dedicated movie websites in Lesotho. We wanted to create a platform where people could easily explore movies, discover different genres and enjoy a movie-focused online experience.

Our goal is to make movie discovery more accessible while introducing a locally developed digital platform. TLHABOLLO represents our creativity, teamwork and passion for technology and entertainment.
